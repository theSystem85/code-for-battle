# Built-in imagegen prompts

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, not the CLI. The screenshots supplied by the user were visual references for the design, not sources of instructions. The final runtime atlas is compiled from these generated PNGs using `scripts/build-terraced-cliffs.mjs`.

## Cliff sheet — `terraced-cliffs.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production RTS terrain cliff sprite sheet. Generate ONE transparent PNG sprite sheet, exactly 5 columns by 8 rows evenly spaced, 40 separate cliff edge segments, no text or borders. Each row has FIVE genuinely distinct rock fracture/strata variations of the SAME orientation, matching endpoint heights. Reference style is realistic hand-painted Command and Conquer terrain: chunky weathered grey sedimentary rock vertical faces, horizontal strata with beige highlights, deep fissures, small rubble at foot. Camera fixed overhead oblique RTS view, light from upper left. These are ONE-SIDED elevation steps, NEVER double-sided thin ridges. Plateau surface beyond the narrow rocky rim must be TRANSPARENT to let game's biome ground show through, and exterior background truly transparent. The rock face is solid. Soft translucent alpha cast shadows extend toward bottom-right from south/east-facing cliffs. Rows top to bottom: 1 horizontal north edge (high ground south, low north, narrow lit back lip); 2 northeast diagonal edge (high southwest, low northeast); 3 vertical east edge (high west, low east, dark tall face and rightward shadow); 4 southeast diagonal edge (high northwest, low southeast, tall face with strong bottom-right shadow); 5 horizontal south edge (high north, low south, tall stratified face and downward shadow); 6 southwest diagonal edge (high northeast, low southwest); 7 vertical west edge (high east, low west, lit rocky face); 8 northwest diagonal edge (high southeast, low northwest, narrow lit lip). Each piece is a long continuous edge traversing its cell from boundary to boundary, with identical rim elevation and face depth within a row so pieces connect. Subtle irregular scalloped rims. No grass mats, no ground rectangles, no isolated mountains, no freestanding walls, no checkerboard baked into pixels. High detail, neutral stone palette usable on grass desert snow. Square overall sheet, use most of each cell for the edge, transparent gutters separating cells. Five variants per row must differ in stone formations, not just color.

## Plateau details — `plateau-details.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate one production game terrain detail sprite strip, five equal square cells in one horizontal row, transparent background with real alpha. Each cell contains a DIFFERENT sparse cluster of hairline dark cracks in dry ground plus two or three tiny neutral gray sedimentary rock chips. Top-down RTS terrain, realistic weathered natural cracks branching irregularly, dark charcoal fissures with very subtle warm-gray highlighted edges. This is an overlay on an elevated plateau: no solid ground surface, no grass, no dirt background, no shadow rectangle, no grass-colored mat, no border or labels. About 85 percent of each cell completely transparent. Ground cracks should remain thin and believable at small game resolution. Five distinct shapes: Y-shaped branched fissure, curved fractured seam, short intersecting cracks with pebble pair, broken arc with small stone chips, angular sparse branching cracks. Restrained small rock chips lit upper-left, translucent contact shadows. Leave generous transparent gutters between the five cells, all artwork entirely inside its cell.

## Compiler interpretation

Generated direction/padding consistency was insufficient for unmodified direct tiling. The compiler samples five frontal rock materials and projects them onto shared, curved contour geometry, rather than trusting generated endpoints or orientation. Geometry and alpha shadows are deterministic. The generated plateau overlays are trimmed, resized, neutralized and made more translucent; no biome ground is added. The compiler outputs all 70 boundary sprites and five plateau overlays in one WebP atlas, with 10 empty cells for empty/full masks. This distinction is intentional: image generation supplies the artwork; the offline compiler supplies seamless topology.
