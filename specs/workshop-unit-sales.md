# Workshop unit sales

Sell mode allows players to sell their own fully repaired units in service range of an operational Vehicle Workshop they own. The refund is 66% of the unit's new purchase price, rounded down to whole credits. Damaged units must finish repairs before they qualify.

Hovering over an eligible unit shows the green sell cursor with the refund above it. Clicking the unit credits the owner and removes the unit without creating wreckage. In multiplayer, the host verifies eligibility and applies the refund to the selling party.
