# Spec 143: AI Center

The AI Center uses the approved [map sprite](../public/images/map/buildings/ai_center_map.webp) and [sidebar art](../public/images/sidebar/ai_center_sidebar.webp). The card appears after **Autonomous Systems**. Queuing and placement require a living Technology Research Center and the usual construction chain.

| Footprint | Cost | Power draw | HP | Armor | Limit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3×3 | $5,500 | 130 | 450 | 2 | 1 per player |

The standard one-yard timer is 4.125 simulation seconds at normal power. A placed AI Center awards one extra star per combat veterancy level-up event for its owner. It adds one star even if one event grants multiple levels. Ore deliveries and direct ledger grants keep their normal awards. The bonus ends when the center is sold or destroyed. Losing the Technology Research Center does not turn off an existing AI Center.

The center does not require an external AI service. It accelerates progress through the existing in-game perk trees. Scripted opponents do not independently research or build this tier.
