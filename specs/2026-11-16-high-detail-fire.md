# Configurable high detail fire

Graphics offers High detail (default) and Low detail. Low preserves the two-path classic animation with alpha. High samples a periodic turbulent flame atlas with transparent edges, smoke, wind shear, and deterministic sparks. Shared-edge samples bridge horizontal and vertical burning neighbours; hidden tiles never contribute bridges. Simulation time drives visuals, so pausing freezes them.

## Rendering decision

A runtime fluid/generative simulation permits rich interactions but needs evolving velocity/density textures, extra passes, GPU bandwidth, device-loss recovery and a separate fallback. A pre-rendered generative field in a sprite atlas gives bounded work, no simulation textures and the same animation on the existing WebGPU terrain + Canvas2D effects overlay and fallback backends. Choose the atlas. This is WebGPU-compatible overlay rendering, not a new native WebGPU compute pass.

`public/images/effects/64x128_8x4_fire.webp` is generated by `node scripts/build-fire-sheet.mjs`, WebP quality 85, alpha blend, with the explosion filename/row-major atlas contract. Frames 0–15 loop flames; 16–31 loop smoke. Atlas decoding starts during renderer asset preparation, and lookup during gameplay never starts loading. Decoded budget is 1 MiB. Missing preparation uses classic fire. No live sprite resizing caches, gradients, particle allocations or map-wide component rebuilding.

Each tile starts at severity 1 and advances every 20 simulation seconds to level 5. Existing persisted age supplies severity without changing save state shape. Severity scales flame width/height, smoke opacity, spark count, damage (base DPS times level), suppression duration (3 seconds times level), and spread probability (base times level, wind weighted, clamped to one). Spread checks retain their five-second interval and cannot cascade in one tick. Suppression stops spread. Apache ground rocket impacts may ignite surviving buildings or flammable vegetation, excluding airborne intercepts and no-building-damage projectiles.

## Validation and performance acceptance

Per frame O(active fires + units), with viewport and visibility culling and constant neighbour lookups. At 1,000 visible tiles, at most 6,000 small atlas quads and 9,000 tiny spark rectangles with bridges; raster cost grows with DPR squared. No map-size translucent buffers or GPU readbacks are added.
