# Situational music director

Music is local presentation only. `src/audio/musicDirector.js` owns event history and hysteresis, reads simulation state without mutating it, and uses no lockstep PRNG. `selectMusicState` is pure. A single frame-loop guard evaluates once per 1,000 simulation milliseconds. Session termination/cache reset or simulation clock rewind clears event history.

Priority: nearDefeat > underAttackOutnumbered > firstAttack > normal.

- Normal retains the nonrepeating random playlist of music01–music05, using Math.random. A timeupdate within 3.5 seconds of the end starts the playlist fade-out of the current track; ended remains the fallback if duration events are unavailable.
- First attack is observed when a local armed unit fires at an enemy or deals positive enemy damage through the common combat damage path. Friendly fire and environmental damage are ignored. It is consumed at most once per game; if a higher priority state intervenes before playback, it stays pending.
- Danger requires positive enemy damage to a local asset in the preceding 10 simulation seconds. At the most recently damaged asset's center, count living combat units within 12 tiles. Danger applies if nearby enemy combat units exceed nearby friendly combat units, or exceed all living local combat units. This is a conservative nearby-attacker proxy, rather than tracking every projectile's shooter. A fixed combat type set includes tanks, artillery, combat aircraft, destroyers, battleships, submarines and carriers; harvesters, mine service units, transports and utility vehicles are excluded. Unit centers use x/y plus half a tile; building centers use tile coordinates and footprint size. No occupancy or collision state is modified.
- Start-of-game guard: near defeat can never trigger until the local player has owned a construction yard or ore refinery at least once in the session (`baseEstablished`) and simulation time is at least `NEAR_DEFEAT_MIN_GAME_TIME_MS` (180,000 ms = 3 minutes). The loading phase and initial state (no buildings, low money) therefore never select the sad track; the director starts in `normal`, so the normal playlist plays at game start. Reset/rewind clears `baseEstablished`.
- Otherwise, near defeat means no living construction yard, either no living refinery or no living harvester, and money below the cheapest currently unlocked unit's configured cost. Unlocked means present in availableUnitTypes, irrespective of affordability or whether a factory survived; no unlocked unit means an infinite recovery cost. Alternatively, at most two living buildings (including initial factory yards), zero combat units, and no active building or unit production across ground/naval/air lanes qualifies. Queued but unstarted orders do not count as producing. Paused orders do not count as active production.

Each situational state dwells at least 50 simulation seconds. Every switch has a 20-second cooldown. Near defeat alone may bypass another state's dwell, after that cooldown. A lower-priority or normal transition also requires 15 continuous seconds without the current condition; recurrence resets that timer. First attack's clear period begins on the following evaluation, so it returns after its dwell in ordinary operation. More urgent conditions preserve pending first attack if it has not yet been selected.

Situational MP3s loop. Transitions are strictly sequential, never overlapping: the current track fades out to silence over `MUSIC_FADE_OUT_MS` (3.5 s, scaled by master volume) and is paused; only then does the incoming track start from 0 at full master volume, with no fade-in. Exactly one music element is audible at any time. A new request during a fade-out only replaces the pending successor; the fade continues. If the outgoing track ends before the ramp completes, the successor starts immediately. A missing/rejected track preserves the existing playlist and retries on subsequent evaluations. Toggle-off and game pause pause the outgoing track and freeze the ramp; resume preserves a disabled music toggle. Termination cancels the fade and pending successor, stops playback and resets local history. Headless mute prevents track loading and playback. The volume ramp is audio wall time, frozen while paused; all state-selection timers are simulation time.

## Validation and performance

Run `npm run test:unit`, `npm run lint:fix:changed`, and `npm run lint`. Focused tests cover selection priority, one-shot attack, dwell, cooldown, continuous clear delay, economic metrics, damage radius, utility exclusion, sequential fade-out without overlap or fade-in, start-of-game near-defeat guard and pause/toggle/termination.

Call frequency: one O(1) guard per rendered frame, O(U+B) inspection once per simulation second, O(1) local event recording per damage/fire event. For 500 units at 75 FPS this adds approximately 500 unit inspections/second, rather than 37,500; DPR 1/2/3 does not multiply the work because no canvas pixels or GPU resources are touched. Fading out adds one 20 Hz audio timer while active, independent of entity count. No per-entity indexed target lookup or normalization is introduced.

Reproduce the CPU-only active-danger diagnostic with `node scripts/performance/musicDirectorBenchmark.mjs`. It reports mean/p95/p99/max CPU milliseconds for 10,000 scans at 40/200/500/2,000 entities and a heap delta (not a GC attribution). It is not a presented-frame benchmark.

Reference-hardware acceptance remains outstanding under performance_improvement.md: three serial before/after runs with cold traversal, repeated fast full-map scrolling, movement, selected layered units, firing, procedural water and realistic armies at unchanged settings. Capture independently measured FPS, p95/p99/max frames and missed 13.333ms deadlines, update/render CPU, actual backend/DPR, GPU timing where available and memory/GC. Headless/software rendering cannot certify 75 presented FPS.
