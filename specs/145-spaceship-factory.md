# Spec 145: Spaceship Factory

The Spaceship Factory uses the approved [map sprite](../public/images/map/buildings/spaceship_factory_map.webp) and [sidebar art](../public/images/sidebar/spaceship_factory_sidebar.webp). Its card appears after **Orbital Engineering**. Queuing and placement require a living Technology Research Center and the ordinary construction chain. The Rocket Launch Base is a separate path and is not a prerequisite.

| Footprint | Cost | Power draw | HP | Armor | Limit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3×3 | $6,500 | 160 | 650 | 2 | 1 per player |

Without the factory bonus, a single Construction Yard at normal power builds it in 4.875 simulation seconds. Once placed, its prefabricated modules multiply the owner's Construction Yard production speed by 1.2. The bonus applies at the start of each new building job and is lost for new jobs when the factory is sold or destroyed. The usual yard and low-power rules still apply, and the factory does not replace the Construction Yard. It keeps working if the Research Center is lost.

The factory does not produce a spaceship in this release. Scripted opponents do not independently research or build this tier.
