- [x] **Bugfix: M2 / light tank drove backwards; canonical unit sprite facing; M2 autocannon rework**
  - The light tank (M2 Bradley-style) hull and turret art faced north while every other unit faces south, so the vehicle looked like it was reversing. Both source images were rotated 180° (q95 sharp-YUV WebP like all unit sprites), `@1x/@2x/@3x` prepared variants regenerated, and `turretMountPoint` (58,109 → 57,127) / `barrelMountPoint.x` (37 → 32) mirrored so the barrel leaves the turret's real gun port.
  - Audit of all unit map images: the mine layer was also north-facing and compensated with `direction + PI/2` in its renderer (its wreck used `- PI/2` and was drawn backwards). The asset was rotated 180° and the renderer now uses the canonical `- PI/2`. Apache, F-22 and F-35 art is north-facing with consistent `+ PI/2` offsets in all their renderers and wrecks; they render correctly and are recorded as a frozen legacy exception (rotating them requires mirroring many jet/rotor/hardpoint anchors and the Apache tilt atlas — follow-up).
  - New `src/rendering/unitSpriteFacing.js` declares the facing of every unit asset; `tests/unit/unitSpriteFacing.test.js` enforces it. The `new-unit` skill gained a mandatory "Canonical facing direction" section.
  - M2 autocannon: no splash damage (impact is visual only, flash radius 0.35 tiles instead of the default 2 tiles), projectile speed 16 px/frame (was 8 via the shared tank path), full intercept lead aiming for moving targets, tighter spread (~0.3°), and rounds fly slightly past the aim point so they can still hit by collision. Damage per hit unchanged.
  - M2 ammo 150 → 195 (+30%). Real M2: 900 × 25 mm (300 ready + 600 stowed), 7 TOW (2 + 5), 2,200 × 7.62 mm coax (800 + 1,400) — FM 3-22.1 Bradley Gunnery. The game has a single ammo pool and no TOW/coax; one game shot ≈ one 4–5 round 25 mm burst gives 180–225, so 195 (+30%) sits in that range.
  - Version 0.58.0.
