- [ ] **Feature: Light Tank (M2 Bradley-style IFV)**
  New `lightTank` unit: $700, 70 HP, light armor (0.8), speed 0.46 (~1.4x Tank V1), 25 mm autocannon (6 damage per round, 1000 ms reload, 150 rounds). Built in the Vehicle Factory and unlocked together with Tank V1. Integrated into production (sidebar button, radial catalog, AI API tech tree, save/load, cheats), combat and targeting, AI production and group behavior, retreat/logistics, track marks, factory roll-out, wrecks, rotation cache, and HUD tooltips. Firing reuses the tank shot at a lower volume.
  Spec: [Light Tank (M2 Bradley-style IFV)](../../specs/light-tank-bradley.md)

## Art
- Generated by Patrick with Grok Imagine: sprite sheet attempt 2 (hull, turret and barrel on blue; attempt 1 was rejected because the hull had the turret and gun baked in) and a 16:9 three-quarter build-button image. Raw sheet: `docs/asset-sources/light-tank-bradley-spritesheet.jpg`.
- Cut-out: chroma key with edge un-mixing and despill (`docs/asset-sources/light-tank-cutout.py`); graded towards the Tank V1 palette, premultiplied Lanczos resize, mild unsharp (`docs/asset-sources/light-tank-build.py`).
- Resolution: the raw parts are 312×590 (hull), 292×344 (turret) and 29×344 (barrel) px. Committed sources are 125×236, 99×117 and 12×83 px; the prepared @3x hull is 45×84 px. Every density, @3x included, is downsampled, so nothing is upscaled.
- The barrel was shortened to its muzzle-side 60% (the breech is hidden under the turret) so the 25 mm gun protrudes realistically. The turret was scaled to 85% so it covers the hull's turret ring without overhanging the hull.
- Build button: the 16:9 image was extended to a square by continuing the sky and ground (the vehicle is not stretched); the horizon is at ~48% height.
