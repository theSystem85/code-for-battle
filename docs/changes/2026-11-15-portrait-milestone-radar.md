- [x] **Improvement: portrait milestone video on the radar**
  In portrait with the condensed or collapsed sidebar, the milestone clip sits on the bottom-right radar box (250×150, 5:3) instead of a centered half-width strip, and locks on with a short signal glitch.
  Spec: none

Condensed and collapsed portrait (`body.mobile-portrait.sidebar-condensed` and `sidebar-collapsed`) place the native milestone overlay on the same rectangle as `#mobileMinimapOverlay.visible`. That box is 250×150, which is 5:3, matching the 960×576 masters. The right edge is `var(--safe-area-right)`. The bottom edge is the safe-area inset plus the portrait action offset: condensed also clears the 96px build bar. Inline left, top, right, bottom, width, and height stay cleared so a later safe-area change can move the clip. The sidebar `#minimap` stays hidden in these layouts, so the clip uses the radar's spawn rectangle rather than a zero-size measurement.

Half the viewport (50vw, 195×146 on a 390px-wide phone) does not match that radar, so it is no longer used. On a 390×844 phone with zero safe-area insets the clip is 250×150 at x=140, y=526 while the sidebar is condensed (bottom offset 168px: 96px bar + 48px actions + 24px gap). With an iPhone home-indicator inset of 34px the same clip sits at y=492. Collapsed drops the build bar, so the bottom offset is 72px plus the safe-area inset (y=622 when the inset is 0). Desktop, landscape, and expanded portrait still follow the live `#minimap` rectangle. Tap-to-close stays on the clip only. The rest of the screen is not covered.

Showing the clip adds `signal-acquire` for 400ms: the picture's colour channels split with a drop-shadow filter, a few horizontal slices shift, and a scanline overlay flickers, then the picture snaps into focus. Closing or ending the clip adds `signal-release` and runs that motion backward during the existing 400ms opacity fade. Both are CSS transforms, filters, clip-path, and opacity on the video and three static overlay spans. Nothing copies video frames and nothing runs per simulation tick. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` skips the classes and keeps the plain opacity fade. The lock plays the registered `uiToggleOn` chirp at volume 0.22. Embedded-audio clips, MP3 clips, crop, radar fade, and the queue are unchanged.
