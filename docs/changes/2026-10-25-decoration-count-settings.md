- [x] **Feature: Map decoration count settings**
  Map Settings gains number inputs for lakes, an overall decorative-tile total, stones, wood clusters, wood-cluster radius, single trees, woods, other vegetation, ground detail, and city density. The overall total is always the sum of stones, single trees, woods, other vegetation and ground detail. Defaults are about 3% of the legacy decoration mix and about 30% of the legacy lake count. Trees and other vegetation prefer shores and lakes.
  Spec: [Decoration count settings](../../specs/2026-10-25-decoration-count-settings.md)
