# Newspaper story intro and landing hero

- [x] **Narrated newspaper intro** Skippable first-play intro pans over "The Global Herald" front page with one ElevenLabs narration clip per section. Spec: [Newspaper story intro](../../specs/newspaper-story-intro.md)
- [x] **Landing story hero** Landing page opens with the newspaper headline and front page, a "How the war began" section and a scrolling news ticker. Spec: [Newspaper story intro](../../specs/newspaper-story-intro.md)
- [x] **Fictional council** The newspaper's UN emblem, attribution and "United Nations" mention were edited (not regenerated) into the fictional Accord Council. Spec: none
- [x] **Intro and first-game music** The first-launch intro plays "First Strike" under the narration (ducked to 25 %); closing it fades First Strike out for the full 3 s, then "Battle Track 02" opens the first gameplay session. Without an intro, Battle Track 02 starts on the first input of the first session. An explicit music-off choice is remembered and respected. The first attack after the intro does not repeat First Strike. Spec: [Newspaper story intro](../../specs/newspaper-story-intro.md)
- [x] **Narrated tutorial** Every tutorial step is voiced by the intro narrator (35 ElevenLabs clips under `public/sound/tutorial/`), with browser TTS as fallback. Spec: [Newspaper story intro](../../specs/newspaper-story-intro.md)
- [x] **Tutorial narration without input choice** Tutorial narration after step 7 fell back to browser TTS when the input-method step was skipped (no preferred method). The tutorial now uses the layout's method (touch or mouse-keyboard) for both copy and narration, and clip lookup ignores whitespace and typographic punctuation, so every step plays its recorded clip. Gamepad and custom key bindings still use browser TTS. Spec: none
- [x] **Intro audio without Sound on button** The intro narration stopped at every section on iOS and needed "Sound on" again each time, and First Strike did not play under it. Narration now plays through one shared, gesture-unlocked Web Audio context; the first tap or key press unlocks narration and music together (that tap does not skip); music is ducked to 35 % instead of 25 %; the Sound on button is removed. Spec: [Newspaper story intro](../../specs/newspaper-story-intro.md)
