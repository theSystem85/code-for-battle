- [x] **Feature: first vehicle factory milestone video with built-in sound**
  Building your first Vehicle Factory now plays a 15 s HEVC clip (960×576, the radar's 5:3 shape) over the minimap. Engine, tracks, workshop hum and the narrator line are inside the video, so there is no companion MP3.

The clip is `public/video/first_factory.mp4` and the `firstFactory` milestone sets `videoFilename: 'first_factory'`. `playSyncedVideoAudio` asks `milestoneClipIsEmbeddedAudioOnly` (a fixed list in `src/ui/milestoneAudioMode.js`, optionally forced with `options.embeddedAudio`) whether the clip has an MP3. For listed clips it passes no audio file, so the overlay never requests an MP3, never waits on a failed load, and plays the video element unmuted. Volume is the narration gain times the master volume and the radar fade, and master mute or headless mute silences it, the same as the MP3 path. If the browser blocks unmuted autoplay the picture starts muted and the existing gesture hook unmutes it. The preload cache also skips the MP3 for these clips. MP3-backed milestones are unchanged.
