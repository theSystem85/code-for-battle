# PR #688 round 3 (2026-10-17, 0.61.0)

Rebased onto main 0.60.0; this PR now ships as 0.61.0 (`features/0.61.0.json`).

- **City HP tenfold lower.** `CITY_BUILDING_HEALTH` is now 150 / 250 / 400 HP for 1 / 2 / 4 tile footprints (was 1500 / 2500 / 4000); other footprints use 100 HP per tile.
- **Normal attack on cities.** `findEnemyTarget` falls back to a living city under the cursor, and `updateEnemyHover` shows the attack cursor over it, so a standard click attacks without force attack. Cities are still not in `gameState.buildings`, so auto-targeting ignores them. Host commands, lockstep, replays and save restore resolve city ids through `findCityBuildingById`.
- **City houses sit on streets.** City clusters reserve one-tile streets on the three-tile block grid, keep them around placed buildings and connect each street component to the existing road network. The streets are stamped as normal `street` tiles (flag `cityStreet`), so they render and path like other roads, and buildings never cover them. Verified in the browser: seed 23, 96x96, 30 city buildings, all 30 touching a street, 239 city street tiles, connected to the main road network (`/workspace/vfx-verify/688-r3/3-city-streets.png`).
